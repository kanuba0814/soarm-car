# Kuruma Explicit Roller Provenance

`meshes/roller.obj` and `model.sdf` are deterministically generated by `scripts/generate-kuruma-rollers.py`. The script reads `artifacts/kuruma.STEP` through OCP 7.9.3.1 before writing output; the STEP is not included in this model.

The visual mesh is a centered capsule envelope reconstructed from the STEP measurement: 38.000 mm overall length and 9.2268 mm maximum radius. Its local `+Z` is the passive roller revolute axis. DART collision uses the equivalent capsule primitive with a 9.2268 mm radius and 19.5464 mm cylindrical section; this preserves the measured overall envelope while avoiding triangle-mesh contact cost. `measurements.json` records the measured ring, spacing, hand, and envelope inputs. Roller/hub masses, inertias, friction, and damping are provisional simulation values, not material or motor measurements.
